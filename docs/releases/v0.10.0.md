# v0.10.0 — Retake one shot, a mirror worth looking at, and a QR that outlives the session

**Download `photobooth-v0.10.0-win-x64.zip`, unzip, run `Photobooth.Server.exe`.**
Nothing to install — no .NET, no Node.

Then open <http://localhost:5000/operator>.

## New in this build

### Retake a single shot

Any one photo can be redone from **review**, before you press Accept. Press
**Retake this one** on its thumbnail and the booth waits for that pose again.

Everything else stays exactly as it was: the other shots keep their photos *and*
their positions, and the replacement drops back into the same slot. Retake shot 2
of 4 and you get a new shot 2, not a resequenced strip.

Both screens name the pose being redone, so a guest reshooting photo two is not
told they are on photo four.

The **Retake** button in the rail is unchanged and still means "the shot taken
most recently" — the two are deliberately separate.

### A bigger posing mirror, with a guide that is finally telling the truth

**The framing box was wrong.** It has claimed to show the strip crop since the
first version and never has — it was hardcoded to 4:3 whatever template was
loaded. On a landscape 6×4 layout the real shape is about 1.67, so guests were
framing themselves against a box that did not match what got printed. It now
comes from the template actually in use.

The mirror was also capped at about half the width of a 1080p screen. It is sized
from the screen now — roughly 1267×713 on a 1080p guest monitor.

The guide is **corner brackets with everything outside them dimmed**, plus a
head-room line and a centre mark. Brackets rather than a body outline, because a
photobooth is mostly groups and an outline of one person tells four people the
wrong thing.

> The guide is still **uncalibrated** — the webcam's field of view has never been
> measured against the R50's. Section 8 of the QA checklist tells you how to
> check it honestly.

### A QR that survives the session

Each session folder now holds a **`qr.png`** — on your machine *and* in the
guest's Drive folder.

The QR on the guest screen is gone the moment the next guest steps in. This is
the copy that answers "we never got our photos" a week later: open the output
folder, find the session, show them the code. It works with the booth switched
off.

It can only exist once there is a Drive link to point at, so sessions captured
with delivery off do not get one.

### A QA checklist

**`QA-CHECKLIST.md`**, in the ZIP. Half a day of structured testing on the
machine and camera you will actually use, starting with the three checks that
could change your plans and ending in a go/no-go. `DRIVE-SETUP.md` is in the ZIP
now too, so whoever configures Google Drive has the instructions to hand.

## Still true

- **Google Drive is off** unless configured. No account, nothing uploaded, and
  the guest screen says to ask for the photos.
- **The app cannot fire the shutter.** The countdown is advisory: the guest sees
  "3-2-1" and you press the remote.
- **Everything about the camera is still an assumption.** File naming,
  extensions, timings — none of it has met a real R50.
- **No printing, no self-serve kiosk.** Neither is built.

## Upgrading from v0.9.x

Unzip alongside the old folder rather than over it, then copy across:

- `data/` — past sessions, your settings, any backdrop image, and the Drive
  sign-in if you have one
- `templates/` — any frame you made in the editor

Older sessions are untouched and their links keep working. They will not gain a
`qr.png` retrospectively; re-publishing one from Setup gives it one.

## Notes

- Windows SmartScreen will warn about an unsigned binary. *More info → Run anyway*.
- You need a **DR-E18 / LP-E17 dummy battery**: the R50 will not power itself over
  USB-C while tethered.
- The version string is at the foot of the left-hand rail; include it with any
  result.
- 181 automated tests pass. None of them involve a camera — that is what the
  field test and the QA checklist are for.
